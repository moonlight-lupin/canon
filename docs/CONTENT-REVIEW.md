# Bundled content: what to review before first use

Canon ships with public-domain hymns, historic creeds and Scripture-based liturgy in English and Simplified Chinese. All Scripture was taken word for word from the KJV and the 和合本 (CUV 1919). Some wordings differ between churches, and a pastor or elder should check them once. Everything below can be edited in **Library**.

## Choose your church's wording

| Text | Bundled wording | Common alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Lord's Prayer (EN) | "forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors" (matches 免我们的债) | "trespasses … those who trespass against us" |
| Apostles' Creed (EN) | "Holy Ghost … the quick and the dead" | "Holy Spirit … the living and the dead" |
| Apostles' Creed (中文) | includes 降在阴间 | omitted or bracketed in some churches |
| Nicene Creed (EN) | 1662 text, modernised spelling, "one holy catholic and apostolic Church" | ELLC 1988 text |
| Gloria Patri (中文) | 荣耀归于圣父、圣子和圣灵；起初这样，现在这样，以后也这样，永无穷尽。阿们。 | 太初如此，现今如此，将来亦如此…… |
| General Confession | BCP 1662, keeps "Christ Jesu" | — |

## Still to add (left out on purpose)

- **Nicene Creed in Chinese.** Several versions circulate (万世以前 / 万世之先 …). Paste the one your church uses.
- **Heidelberg Catechism.** No English wording could be confirmed as public domain.
- **Chinese for the BCP General Confession, the Westminster Shorter Catechism and the BCP collect.** There is no established version we could rely on.
- **Chinese hymn words.** Only the Doxology (三一颂) and Gloria Patri (荣耀颂) have Chinese words. Ten hymns have their Chinese title only. Add the words your congregation sings, from your hymnal under its licence.

## Have a Chinese-speaking pastor read these

The following texts were written for Canon in both languages: the invocation, prayer for illumination, offertory prayer, Lord's Supper invitation and fencing of the table, and baptism vows. The vows use 他/她 and 某某 as the name placeholder.

## Hymn variants worth checking against your hymnal

| Hymn | Wording or detail to check |
|---|---|
| Joyful, Joyful | "chanting bird" or "singing bird" |
| Rock of Ages | "Save from wrath and make me pure" (the common text) or Toplady's original |
| Before the Throne | Bancroft's "bleeding Lamb" and "One with himself" (bundled), or the modern "risen Lamb" |
| Thine Be the Glory | "meets us" or "meets thee" |
| Praise to the Lord | "Now to his temple draw near" (an altered wording) |
| Crown Him with Many Crowns | which stanzas to include |
| Tune and meter details | ALL TO CHRIST, CRIMOND (attributed to Jessie Irvine), and the meter for Before the Throne |
